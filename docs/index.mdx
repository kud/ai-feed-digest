---
title: 👋 Introduction
description: A self-hosted daily briefing that reads your RSS feeds, summarises them with a language model and publishes each edition as a small Next.js site.
---

**ai-feed-digest** turns a list of RSS feeds into one dated edition a day: every fresh article gets a short abstract and a few bullet points, and the day as a whole gets an overview, an analysis and a timeline. Editions are plain Markdown files in `content/editions/`, so the archive is just a folder you can keep in git.

The model calls go through [opencode](https://opencode.ai), which means any model opencode can reach (set as `provider/model`) can write your digest.
